Connect Telegram Group

Connect your Telegram group to RACAAL AI so the group can use its own settings, AI features and management tools.

How to connect your group

  1. Add the RACAAL Telegram bot to your Telegram group.
  2. Give the bot the administrator permissions it needs to manage the enabled group features.
  3. Return to this Control Center after the bot has joined.
  4. The Telegram group will then be registered with its own group ID and settings.
  5. Once registered, all settings below will belong only to that Telegram group.
Important: Each Telegram group must have its own configuration. Do not share one group's settings with another group.
