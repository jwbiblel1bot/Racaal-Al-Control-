Racaal AI Engine

This is the central AI engine for the Racaal platform.

The AI Engine will eventually support:

- Website AI Assistant
- Telegram AI
- WhatsApp AI
- Facebook AI
- Bible knowledge
- Jobs
- Love and Marriage
- Local Search
- Community services
- General question answering
- AI customization
- Customer-specific AI configurations
- Safety and response controls

The AI Engine will communicate with the central backend API.

The existing standalone AI Engine will be connected here carefully without disturbing the stable Telegram bot.
