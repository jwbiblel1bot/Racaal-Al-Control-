Racaal Bible Studies

The Bible Studies module manages Bible-based study material and study activities within the Racaal platform.

Planned functions:

- Bible study topics
- Bible study lessons
- Bible study references
- Bible book studies
- Question-and-answer studies
- Individual Bible studies
- Group Bible studies
- Study progress
- Study schedules
- Study customization
- JW.org source references
- AI-assisted Bible study

The Bible Studies module will remain independent from:

- Bible Games
- Bible Questions
- Bible Topics
- Bible Quizzes

It will use the Bible Module source layer for JW.org references and will eventually connect with:

- Racaal Website
- Telegram
- WhatsApp
- Facebook
- Racaal AI Engine
- Central Backend API
