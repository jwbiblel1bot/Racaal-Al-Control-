Racaal Bible-Based AI Assistance

The Bible-Based AI Assistance module provides AI-powered assistance for Bible-related questions, topics, studies, games, and references.

The AI should use the Racaal Bible Module and its configured Bible knowledge and source system.

Primary external Bible source and reference platform:

- JW.org

Planned functions:

- Answer Bible-related questions
- Explain Bible verses
- Explain Bible topics
- Assist with Bible studies
- Generate Bible questions
- Assist with Bible games
- Assist with Bible quizzes
- Find related Bible references
- Provide JW.org references and links
- Understand Bible-related conversations
- Respond to follow-up Bible questions
- Support group conversations
- Support private conversations
- AI customization by group
- AI customization by channel
- Enable/Disable Bible AI
- AI response controls
- Source and reference controls
- Safety controls

The Bible AI module will connect to the central Racaal AI Engine rather than creating a separate AI engine.

Connection architecture:

User
↓
Channel
↓
Central Backend API
↓
Bible Module
↓
Bible Knowledge & References
↓
Racaal AI Engine
↓
Bible response + appropriate JW.org reference
↓
User

Supported channels:

- Racaal Website
- Telegram
- WhatsApp
- Facebook

The module must keep Bible functionality separate from the other Racaal platform modules while allowing the central AI Engine and API to serve multiple modules.

JW.org content should be handled according to the applicable JW.org terms and permissions. Where appropriate, the system should link users to the original JW.org material rather than copying or redistributing protected publications.
